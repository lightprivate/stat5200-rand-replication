Why two Table 2 numbers need two-step rounding (checked 2026-11-14)

Summary
Two published numbers differ from direct rounding of my estimates:
the inpatient SE for the 50% plan (265) and the inpatient joint p-value (0.1540).
Both match if the numbers are first rounded the way Stata's esttab does by default,
and then rounded again to the decimals shown in the paper. With this rule, all 78 numbers match.
No regression result was changed to get this match.

What is confirmed
- The authors' Table 2 code (original/tables.do, lines 304-366) exports with esttab
  and does not set a number format (line 363). So esttab's default format, a3, applies.
- a3 keeps at least 4 significant digits for numbers above 1, and 3 for numbers below 1.
  Docs: https://repec.sowi.unibe.ch/stata/estout/help-esttab.html
  Source: https://github.com/benjann/estout/blob/master/esttab.ado (default a3 at line 549;
  SEs inherit it at line 644) and estout.ado (lines 428 and 3890-3933).
  Line numbers were checked on the date above and may change.
- a3 was already the default in 2007, before the paper:
  https://www.soz.unibe.ch/ueber_uns/personen/jann/publications_of_ben_jann/e131246/e131267/esttab_ger.pdf

What is guessed
- The second rounding step (to the paper's decimals). I do not know who did it or how.

What I do not have
- The authors' intermediate output, their esttab version, or a run of their Stata code.
